Engineering Mathematics-I

Calculus, Differential Equations, Matrices and important AKTU concepts.

Simple Explanation:

Differential calculus ka use kisi quantity ke change ko measure karne ke liye hota hai.

Important:
Derivative of xⁿ = n xⁿ⁻¹
Derivative of sin x = cos x
Derivative of cos x = -sin x
Simple Explanation:

Jab function mein x, y, z jaise multiple variables ho aur ek variable ke respect mein derivative nikalna ho, ise partial differentiation kehte hain.

Example:
z = x² + y²
∂z/∂x = 2x
∂z/∂y = 2y
