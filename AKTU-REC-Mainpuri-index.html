Engineering Mathematics-I

Calculus, Differential Equations, Matrices and important AKTU concepts.

Simple Explanation:

Differential calculus ka use kisi quantity ke change ko measure karne ke liye hota hai.

Important:
Derivative of xⁿ = n xⁿ⁻¹
Derivative of sin x = cos x
Derivative of cos x = -sin x
Simple Explanation:

Jab function mein x, y, z jaise multiple variables ho aur ek variable ke respect mein derivative nikalna ho, ise partial differentiation kehte hain.

Example:
z = x² + y²
∂z/∂x = 2x
∂z/∂y = 2y

Matrix numbers ko rows aur columns ke form mein arrange karne ka method hai.

Important topics:
• Addition
• Multiplication
• Determinant
• Inverse
• Rank

Differential equation mein unknown function aur uske derivatives present hote hain.

Multiple integration ka use area, volume aur multivariable functions ke calculations mein hota hai.

Basic Electrical Engineering

Electrical fundamentals for B.Tech students.

Ohm's law ke according voltage, current aur resistance ke beech relation V = IR hota hai.

Kirchhoff laws electrical circuits ko analyse karne ke liye use hote hain.

KCL → Current law
KVL → Voltage law

Alternating current time ke saath direction aur magnitude change karta hai.

Transformer AC voltage ko step-up ya step-down karne ke liye use hota hai.

Motors aur generators electrical machines ke important examples hain.
