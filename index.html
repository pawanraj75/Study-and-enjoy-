ℹ️ About AKTU Study Hub

AKTU Study Hub is a comprehensive, student-focused study platform exclusively created for 1st Year engineering students at D.A.H. Rajkiya Engineering College Mainpuri.

Our platform organizes all first-year academic materials into one seamless, internal portal. Access unit-wise notes, previous year question banks, revision formula sheets, short notes, interactive self-assessment tests, and AI-assisted doubt clearing—all operating completely inside this hub with zero external redirections.
