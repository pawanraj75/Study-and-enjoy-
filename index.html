Engineering Mathematics (BAS-103)

Unit 1: Matrices & Linear Algebra

Cayley-Hamilton Theorem: Every square matrix satisfies its own characteristic equation $|A - \lambda I| = 0$. Used to find $A^{-1}$ and higher powers of matrices.

Rank of Matrix: Maximum number of linearly independent rows or columns. Reduced Echelon Form helps in finding Rank.

Unit 2: Differential Calculus I & II

Leibnitz Theorem: Used for finding the $n^{th}$ derivative of product of two functions: $(uv)_n = u_n v + n u_{n-1} v_1 + \frac{n(n-1)}{2!} u_{n-2} v_2 + ...$

Euler's Theorem for Homogeneous Functions: If $u$ is a homogeneous function of degree $n$, then $x\frac{\partial u}{\partial x} + y\frac{\partial u}{\partial y} = nu$.

Unit 3: Multivariable Calculus & Taylor's Series

Expansion of $f(x,y)$ around point $(a,b)$ using Taylor's Theorem. Maxima & Minima condition for two variables: $rt - s^2 > 0$ and $r < 0$ (Maximum), $r > 0$ (Minimum).

Engineering Physics (BAS-101)

Unit 1: Relativistic Mechanics

Michelson-Morley Experiment: Proved the non-existence of hypothetical medium 'Ether' and established speed of light is constant in all inertial frames.

Einstein's Mass-Energy Relation: $E = mc^2$. Length contraction: $L = L_0 \sqrt{1 - v^2/c^2}$.

Unit 3: Quantum Mechanics

De-Broglie Hypothesis: Dual nature of matter. Wavelength $\lambda = \frac{h}{p} = \frac{h}{mv}$.

Schrodinger's Time-Independent Wave Equation: $\frac{d^2\psi}{dx^2} + \frac{8\pi^2 m}{h^2}(E - V)\psi = 0$.

Programming for Problem Solving - C (BCS-101)

Unit 1 & 2: C Basics & Loops

Data Types: int (2/4 bytes), float (4 bytes), char (1 byte).

Loops: for, while (Entry-controlled) vs do-while (Exit-controlled).

Unit 4: Pointers & Dynamic Memory

Pointers: Variable holding memory address of another variable. Syntax: int *ptr = &a;

Dynamic Memory Allocation: malloc(), calloc(), realloc(), free() from <stdlib.h>.

Basic Electrical Engg. (BEE-101)

Unit 1: DC Circuit Theorems

Thevenin's Theorem: Any linear network can be replaced by a single voltage source $V_{th}$ in series with $R_{th}$.

Norton's Theorem: Dual of Thevenin's theorem. Replaced by current source $I_N$ in parallel with $R_N$.

Mechanical Engg. (BME-101)

Unit 1 & 2: Thermodynamics & IC Engines

1st Law of Thermodynamics: Energy can neither be created nor destroyed ($dQ = dU + dW$).

2-Stroke vs 4-Stroke Engines: 2-stroke completes cycle in 1 revolution of crankshaft; 4-stroke requires 2 revolutions.

Engineering Chemistry (BAS-102)

Unit 4: Water Technology

Hardness of Water: Caused by dissolved salts of Ca and Mg. Expressed in ppm or mg/L.

Zeolite Process: Used for softening water by exchanging $Na^+$ ions with $Ca^{2+}$ and $Mg^{2+}$ ions.
